Why we built this

Most PDF tools online make you sign up, limit your file size, put watermarks on your output, or charge you after a "free trial." That's frustrating — especially when you just need to compress one file or merge two documents quickly.

PDFQuick is different. Every tool is completely free, with no account required and no hidden limits. We process everything in your browser using modern JavaScript libraries — so your files never leave your device and we never see your documents.

Technology

PDFQuick is built with vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript — no frameworks, no backend servers. Your files are processed entirely on your machine using these excellent open-source libraries:

📄 pdf-lib 👁️ pdf.js (Mozilla) 🔍 Tesseract.js (OCR) 📊 PptxGenJS 📝 docx.js 🗜️ JSZip 📄 mammoth.js
