Edit Text mode: click any existing text on the page to edit it in place. Best for short fixes (names, dates, numbers, single words/lines) — since this covers the old text rather than reflowing the page, typing something much longer or shorter than the original may not align perfectly with surrounding text.

⚠️ Privacy note: this visually covers the original text with a matching background and draws your new text on top — it does not erase the old text from the file's internal data. If you're removing sensitive information (not just fixing a typo), use the Redact PDF tool instead, which permanently deletes the content.
