Protect PDF

Add a password to your PDF and control what readers can do — print, copy, edit.

🔒
Privacy first: Your PDF is encrypted entirely in your browser using real RC4-128 PDF encryption. It is never sent to any server. When someone opens the protected PDF, their PDF reader will prompt for the password.

Note on permissions: "Allow readers to" restrictions (print, copy, etc.) are honored by full PDF applications like Adobe Acrobat/Reader and Foxit. Lightweight built-in viewers (Chrome, Edge, Firefox) typically only check the password and don't enforce these flags — that's a limitation of those viewers, not of the protection applied here.
