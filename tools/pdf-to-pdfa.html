What is PDF/A?

1

ISO Standard

PDF/A is an ISO-standardized version of PDF for electronic document preservation.

2

Self-contained

All fonts, color profiles, and metadata are embedded — no external dependencies.

3

Long-term

Designed to be reliably reproduced in the future, regardless of software changes.

4

Compliance

Required by many governments, courts, and regulatory bodies worldwide.
