Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2025

🔒 Short version: Your files never leave your device. PDFQuick processes all PDFs directly in your browser. We do not upload, store, or have access to any of your documents.

1. Who we are

PDFQuick is a free online PDF tool suite. We provide tools to compress, merge, split, sign, and convert PDF files, all running entirely in your web browser.

2. Files and documents

All file processing on PDFQuick happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. This means:

  • Your PDF files are never uploaded to our servers.
  • We have no access to the content of your documents.
  • Files are processed in memory and are gone the moment you close the tab.
  • We cannot recover or view any file you process on our site.

3. Information we collect

We collect minimal, anonymous data to improve the service:

  • Usage analytics: Which tools are used, how often, and general browser/device type (via Google Analytics). No personal data is collected.
  • Error reporting: Anonymous error logs if a tool crashes, to help us fix bugs.
  • No account data: We do not require you to sign up. We do not collect your name, email, or any personal information.

4. Cookies

We use cookies only for:

  • Google Analytics (anonymous usage statistics)
  • Google AdSense (advertisements shown to free users)

You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time. The tools will continue to work without cookies.

5. Advertising

PDFQuick is free and supported by advertising. We use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may use cookies to show relevant ads based on your browsing history. You can opt out of personalised ads at adssettings.google.com.

6. Third-party services

PDFQuick uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Analytics — anonymous site usage tracking
  • Google AdSense — advertising
  • Google Fonts — typography (Inter typeface)
  • CDN libraries — pdf-lib, pdf.js, Tesseract.js (open source, loaded from public CDNs)

7. Children's privacy

PDFQuick does not knowingly collect any information from children under 13. The service does not collect personal data from any user.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date. Continued use of PDFQuick after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, please visit our About page for contact information.
