Is my electronic signature legally valid?

For the vast majority of everyday documents — NDAs, rental agreements, job offer letters, purchase orders — an electronic signature is fully legally binding in most countries:

  • USA: ESIGN Act (2000) and UETA make e-signatures legally equivalent to handwritten ones.
  • European Union: eIDAS Regulation recognises electronic signatures across all EU member states.
  • UK: Electronic Communications Act 2000 gives e-signatures legal effect.
  • India: Information Technology Act 2000 validates digital signatures.

⚠️ Exceptions: Some documents require a qualified or certified digital signature with a cryptographic certificate — for example, certain government filings, notarised documents, and wills. Check the requirements for your specific document type.
