Why merge PDFs?

The most common reasons people combine PDFs:

  • Job applications — combine CV, cover letter, and certificates into one file.
  • Reports and presentations — join chapters, appendices, or scanned exhibits.
  • Contracts — merge multiple agreement pages or annexures.
  • Scanned documents — individual page scans combined into a single file.
  • Email attachments — reduce multiple attachments to one clean PDF.

Step-by-step: merge PDFs with PDFQuix

  1. Go to PDFQuix → Merge PDF.
  2. Click Add Files or drag and drop your PDFs onto the upload zone. You can add up to 20 files.
  3. Reorder the files by dragging them into the sequence you want.
  4. Click Merge PDF. Your browser handles everything — no file is uploaded to any server.
  5. Click Download to save your combined PDF.

💡 Tip: You can rearrange the order of files before merging by dragging them up or down in the list. The first file in the list becomes page 1 of the merged document.

Does merging affect quality?

Not at all. Merging PDFs is a completely lossless operation. The content of each file — text, images, fonts, form fields, annotations — is preserved exactly as-is. Nothing is re-encoded or compressed during the merge.

The final file size is roughly the sum of the individual file sizes (sometimes slightly smaller due to shared font data being deduplicated).

Tips for better results

  • Compress first if size matters — run each PDF through Compress PDF before merging, especially for image-heavy files.
  • Rotate pages before merging — fix any landscape/portrait issues first with Rotate PDF.
  • Page numbers — after merging, use Add Page Numbers to number all pages sequentially.

Frequently asked questions

Can I merge a PDF with a Word document?

You'll need to convert the Word file to PDF first. Use Word to PDF to convert it, then merge both PDFs.

Is there a file size limit?

No server-side limit — PDFQuix processes files entirely in your browser. Your device's RAM is the only real constraint.

Does PDFQuix store my files?

No. Your files are processed locally in your browser. We never receive or store them.
