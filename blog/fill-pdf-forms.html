How to fill an interactive PDF form

  1. Open PDFQuix → Fill Forms.
  2. Upload your PDF form.
  3. All interactive fields are highlighted automatically. Click any field to start typing.
  4. Use Tab to move to the next field.
  5. Check checkboxes and radio buttons by clicking them.
  6. For signature fields, click to open the signature pad and draw, type, or upload your signature.
  7. Click Download Filled PDF.

💡 Tip: After downloading, open the PDF and scroll through every page to verify all fields are filled correctly before sending.

How to fill a scanned or static form

If the form doesn't have interactive fields (it's a scan or a flat PDF), use the Annotate PDF tool instead:

  1. Open Annotate PDF and upload the form.
  2. Select the Text Box tool.
  3. Click over each blank line or box and type your answer.
  4. Resize and reposition text boxes to align perfectly with the form fields.
  5. Download the annotated PDF.

Common form types and tips

  • Tax forms (e.g., ITR, SA100, W-9): Usually interactive PDFs — fill directly. Double-check all figures before signing.
  • Job applications: Often interactive. Add your signature last.
  • Visa / government forms: Usually interactive. Save a copy before submission.
  • Tenancy agreements: May be static — use annotate mode for text boxes, then e-Sign.

⚠️ Before you submit: Some forms require a "wet" (physical) signature even if they look like PDFs. Check the submission instructions — not all PDF forms can be submitted electronically.

Frequently asked questions

Can I save my progress and come back later?

Yes — download the partially filled PDF at any time and re-open it in PDFQuix to continue filling. Your entries are saved in the file itself.

Can I clear all form fields and start over?

Use the Reset Fields button to clear all entries and start fresh.

What if a required field won't let me leave it blank?

PDFQuix doesn't enforce field validation — you can download even if required fields are empty. Use your judgment to ensure the form is complete before submitting.
