How to Add a Watermark to a PDF Free

✍️ PDFQuix Team 📅 May 2025 ⏱️ 4 min read

Watermarks serve two main purposes: branding (stamping your logo or company name on documents you share) and status labelling (marking drafts, confidential materials, or proofs). You can do both on any PDF in seconds — free, with no account needed.

How to add a text watermark

  1. Open PDFQuix → Watermark PDF.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Select Text Watermark.
  4. Type your watermark text (e.g., "CONFIDENTIAL" or "DRAFT").
  5. Customise: choose font size, colour, opacity (30–50% is typical for a subtle watermark), and rotation angle (45° diagonal is the classic look).
  6. Select position: centre, tile across page, or custom position.
  7. Choose which pages to watermark (all pages, first page only, or a range).
  8. Click Apply Watermark and download.

💡 Opacity tip: 30–40% opacity for diagonal text watermarks is the sweet spot — visible enough to communicate the status, subtle enough not to obscure the content.

How to add a logo / image watermark

  1. Open PDFQuix → Watermark PDF.
  2. Upload your PDF.
  3. Select Image Watermark.
  4. Upload your logo. PNG with a transparent background gives the cleanest result — the background of the PDF shows through.
  5. Set the size (as a percentage of page width) and position (corner, centre, or custom).
  6. Adjust opacity if you want a subtle ghost watermark rather than a full-opacity logo.
  7. Click Apply Watermark and download.

Common watermark use cases

  • DRAFT — Mark documents still under review so recipients don't treat them as final.
  • CONFIDENTIAL — Legal, HR, and financial documents shared on a need-to-know basis.
  • SAMPLE — Proposals, portfolios, or design proofs sent for approval before final delivery.
  • DO NOT COPY — Proprietary materials, exam papers, or exclusive content.
  • Logo watermark — Brand protection on shared reports, whitepapers, or client deliverables.
  • © Copyright notice — Photography, artwork, or creative work shared in PDF format.

⚠️ Watermarks are not security. A determined recipient with PDF editing software can remove watermarks. For genuinely confidential documents, combine watermarking with password protection and redaction of sensitive fields.

Frequently asked questions

Can I remove a watermark I added with PDFQuix?

Not from the downloaded file — watermarks are permanently embedded. Always keep your original unwatermarked PDF. If you need to remove a watermark from a third-party PDF, the Redact tool can black out a region, but it won't cleanly "erase" a diagonal text watermark.

What format should my logo be in?

PNG with a transparent background is ideal. If you only have a JPG or a logo on a white background, the white box will be visible on the PDF. Convert to PNG with transparency first if possible.

Does watermarking change the file size?

Very slightly — a few kilobytes for text watermarks, a bit more for image watermarks. Not a meaningful difference for most documents.
