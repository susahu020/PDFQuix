How to convert PDF to Word with PDFQuix

  1. Go to PDFQuix → PDF to Word.
  2. Drop your PDF or click to select it.
  3. Click Convert to Word. Processing happens in your browser.
  4. Download the .docx file and open it in Microsoft Word or Google Docs.

How to open a PDF directly in Microsoft Word

If you have Microsoft Word 2013 or later, you can open a PDF directly:

  1. Open Word → File → Open.
  2. Browse to your PDF file and open it.
  3. Word will display a message: "Word will now convert your PDF to an editable Word document." Click OK.
  4. The PDF opens as an editable document. Save as .docx.

This works well for simple, text-heavy PDFs. Complex layouts with columns or tables may need manual cleanup.

How to convert using Google Docs (free)

  1. Go to Google Drive and upload your PDF.
  2. Right-click the PDF → Open with → Google Docs.
  3. Google converts the PDF to a Docs file. Then File → Download → Microsoft Word (.docx).

⚠️ Note: Uploading to Google Drive means your file is stored on Google's servers. Avoid this for confidential documents.

When conversion quality is poor

PDF-to-Word conversion isn't perfect for every document. Here's when to expect issues and what to do:

  • Scanned PDFs — These are images, not text. Run OCR first: PDFQuix → OCR PDF, then convert.
  • Complex multi-column layouts — May need manual reformatting in Word.
  • Custom fonts — May be substituted if the font isn't available on your system.
  • Charts and diagrams — Convert as images; underlying data won't be editable.
