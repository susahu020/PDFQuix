How to batch process PDFs

  1. Open PDFQuick → Batch Process.
  2. Select the operation you want to apply (Compress, Convert, Rotate, etc.).
  3. Click Add Files and select up to 20 PDFs, or drag them all at once into the upload zone.
  4. Configure the operation settings (e.g., compression level, output format).
  5. Click Process All. Your browser processes each file in parallel.
  6. Download individual files or click Download All as ZIP.

💡 Large batches: For 50+ files, split into groups of 20. Process the first batch, download the ZIP, then repeat. Your browser performance stays smooth with 20 files at a time.

Real-world use cases

  • Archive compression: Compressing a year's worth of invoice PDFs from 2GB to 400MB.
  • Client deliverables: Watermarking 15 design proof PDFs in one go.
  • OCR processing: Making 10 scanned documents searchable simultaneously.
  • Format conversion: Converting a folder of Word documents to PDF for a client portal.
  • Image export: Extracting cover pages as JPGs from 20 report PDFs.

ℹ️ Performance note: All processing happens on your device. More CPU cores and RAM = faster batch processing. Close other browser tabs to free up resources for large batches.

Downloading your results

After processing, you have two download options:

  • Individual downloads: Click the download icon next to each file to download them one at a time.
  • Download All as ZIP: Get all processed files in a single .zip archive — useful for large batches.
