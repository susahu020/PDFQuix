iLovePDF vs PDFQuix: Which Is Better for Privacy?

✍️ PDFQuix Team 📅 May 2025 ⏱️ 6 min read

iLovePDF is one of the most popular free PDF tools online, with millions of monthly users. PDFQuix offers a similar set of features. The most important difference between them isn't tools or design — it's where your files go.

The privacy difference explained

When you use iLovePDF, your PDF is uploaded to their servers in Spain. Their privacy policy states they delete files after processing, but the file does travel across the internet to a third-party server.

When you use PDFQuix, your file is processed entirely by JavaScript running in your browser tab. The PDF bytes never leave your computer — no upload, no server, no third-party access. PDFQuix cannot see your files even if it wanted to.

ℹ️ When does privacy matter? For public brochures and general documents, either tool is fine. For contracts, payslips, medical records, legal documents, or anything with PII — local processing is the only genuinely private option.

Speed comparison

iLovePDF: Depends on your internet connection (upload speed) and server load. Fast internet = fast processing. Slow internet = slow upload + slow download.

PDFQuix: Depends on your device's CPU. No network time at all. On a modern laptop or phone, most operations complete in 2–10 seconds regardless of internet speed. Works on a plane in aeroplane mode.

When to choose iLovePDF

  • You need a tool PDFQuix doesn't offer (iLovePDF has more niche tools).
  • You're on a very old, slow device where local processing is sluggish.
  • The document is non-sensitive and you want a mobile app experience.

When to choose PDFQuix

  • Privacy matters — the document contains personal, financial, or confidential information.
  • You have slow internet but a capable device.
  • You want genuinely unlimited use with no daily operation caps.
  • You want free batch processing.
  • You work offline or in a restricted network environment.

Frequently asked questions

Is iLovePDF safe?

iLovePDF is a legitimate, established company with a clear privacy policy. "Safe" depends on your definition: they don't sell your data or use files inappropriately, but files do pass through their servers. For sensitive documents, "safe" should mean the file never leaves your device.

Is PDFQuix really free with no limits?

Yes. No daily limits, no file size limits (beyond your device's RAM), no watermarks, no account needed. PDFQuix is ad-supported, which is how it remains free.
